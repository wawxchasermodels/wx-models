Interstate (MUTCD M1-1) and US route (MUTCD M1-4) markers are public-domain
artwork from Wikimedia Commons (PD-USGov-MUTCD), rendered with the same
dark halo and light rim as the page-graphic renderer (mapgen/render_alert_map.py).
State route markers are MUTCD M1-5 style (white circle for 1-2 digits, white
rounded rectangle for 3 digits) generated by that same renderer so they match
the shields on alert graphics posted to the Facebook pages.
