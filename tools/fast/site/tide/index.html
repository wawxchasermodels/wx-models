Data: NOAA CO-OPS tide predictions and water levels · NOAA STOFS-2D-Global guidance (public domain) · Conditional Prediction is experimental, not an official forecast

Official prediction and observed water level: NOAA CO-OPS Data API (api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov), datum MLLW, feet. Flood levels: the official NWS minor, moderate and major coastal flood levels from the CO-OPS metadata API, converted to MLLW and drawn only where they match the values cross-checked against the Tides & Currents Coastal Inundation Dashboard and the NWS National Water Prediction Service (verified Nov 14, 2026). Birch Bay has no NOAA gauge of its own and uses Cherry Point (9449424).

Conditional Prediction (experimental, default): the official NOAA tide prediction, plus the effect of air pressure (lower pressure raises the water; about 1 cm per hPa). For hours already past we use the pressure measured at the gauge; for the hours ahead we use a forecast from the European AIFS ensemble. A warm-water adjustment uses sea-surface temperature compared with a typical year at this station. A correction from the last few days of gauge readings holds the leftover after pressure and warm water are taken out, and a 12-hour fade starts the line at the latest observation. The default does not use the NOAA surge model. The Wind surge chip (off by default) adds only the wind-driven part of that model, without applying the pressure or warm-water terms a second time. Experimental, not official. NOAA does not endorse this site.

A daily self-calibration (10-day verification by forecast range, shrunk, capped at ±0.5 ft) is computed and archived in the background for evaluation; it is not applied to the chart or the stats.

Conditional, verified (dashed): every 6 h (02/08/14/20 UTC) the Conditional Prediction is archived per station; for each past time the line shows the newest snapshot issued at least the chosen lead earlier, beside the observed line. Stats: mean absolute error and bias over hourly points (forecast − observed) and the miss at the highest observed high tide, for the Conditional and the official prediction over the same hours. Before the live archive began the snapshots are a hindcast: recomputed afterwards with the previous Conditional method, using only observations up to the issue time and the newest NOAA surge-model cycle posted by then. The live amber line is the newer default and does not use that surge model unless Wind surge is on.
