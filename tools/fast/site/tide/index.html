Data: NOAA CO-OPS tide predictions and water levels · NOAA STOFS-2D-Global guidance (public domain) · Conditional Prediction is experimental, not an official forecast

Official prediction and observed water level: NOAA CO-OPS Data API (api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov), datum MLLW, feet. Flood levels: the official NWS minor, moderate and major coastal flood levels from the CO-OPS metadata API, converted to MLLW and drawn only where they match the values cross-checked against the Tides & Currents Coastal Inundation Dashboard and the NWS National Water Prediction Service (verified Nov 14, 2026). Birch Bay has no NOAA gauge of its own and uses Cherry Point (9449424).

Conditional Prediction (experimental, default): official NOAA tide prediction plus the inverse-barometer (low pressure) effect of about 1 cm per hPa versus 1013.25 hPa, plus a warm-water term equal to only the recent SST change since the window STOFS last averaged (newest 2 days of NOAA OISST anomaly minus the prior 5 days, times a per-station coefficient; the slow 30-day steric rise is already in STOFS's 5-day station bias and is not added on top), fading in over 12 h, plus a fading correction to the current observed residual so the line matches the latest observation and that correction fades over 12 h. Forecast sea-level pressure is the ECMWF AIFS ensemble mean (open data, nearest 0.25° grid point, interpolated to hourly); observed CO-OPS pressure is used for past and present hours. If AIFS ENS mean MSL is missing for a run or lead, the page falls back to AIFS single or IFS and says so. Both modes keep the current Conditional's STOFS 5-day station anomaly (combined water level minus the no-anomaly run), fading 12 h residual, and 30-day offset, applied once, plus an AIFS-minus-GFS inverse-barometer swap and the version D warm-water term, both starting at 0 at the newest observation. The Wind surge chip (off by default) adds only the wind-driven part of NOAA STOFS-2D-Global: STOFS surge minus that 5-day anomaly minus the inverse barometer computed on STOFS's own GFS/UFS forcing pressure, not on the AIFS pressure, so the low-pressure term and the anomaly are never applied twice. STOFS is barotropic and does not model steric or sea-surface temperature. NOAA does not endorse this site.

A daily self-calibration (10-day verification by forecast range, shrunk, capped at ±0.5 ft) is computed and archived in the background for evaluation; it is not applied to the chart or the stats.

Conditional, verified (dashed): every 6 h (02/08/14/20 UTC) the Conditional Prediction is archived per station; for each past time the line shows the newest snapshot issued at least the chosen lead earlier, beside the observed line. Stats: mean absolute error and bias over hourly points (forecast − observed) and the miss at the highest observed high tide, for the Conditional and the official prediction over the same hours. Before the live archive began the snapshots are a hindcast: recomputed afterwards with the same method, using only observations up to the issue time and the newest STOFS cycle posted by then.
